Being honest with yourself and with others is not easy, because it involves asking yourself what it really means to be honest, how to be honest, and when it is convenient. 

The best thing would be: yes, be honest in everything you do, but to what extent is it convenient, in my case I try to be as honest as possible with my friends, with my family, with my social group, but it is really difficult for me, it may be a skill that should be practiced, because I do not believe that honesty is simply honesty and forgetting about everything else, no. 

Honesty implies that you must always act with good intentions, so pure honesty is not enough, and always acting with good intentions can be difficult because it is a constant that we have to keep in mind especially in difficult moments, when your mind is telling you bad things about yourself and your resilience comes into action.

In fact, I'm not the only one who thinks about this, but also the book by [stop being you](https://www.amazon.com/dp/8479538252?lv=shuf&language=es&channelId=500&plpRedirect=mhFallback) mentions that many times we have two personalities, and we have to learn how to unify ourselves into a single person through meditation. 

So yes, this is a short essay, I just wanted to mention a little bit about honesty is one of the most important things that we have to count on in this life to be able to have better relationships with people and to be able to really be a good team.
